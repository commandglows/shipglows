# Value-Led Trial Transition Experience Contract

## Outcome

Extend the existing product-entitlement support and proof branch so every Glows product can turn the full end-of-trial journey into calm awareness, recognition of experienced value, a clear decision, and trustworthy recovery without creating a competing UX or marketing authority.

## Contract

- Entitlement truth determines access; UX never infers or grants it.
- The product remains interruption-free before its configured reminder window.
- Reminder milestones derive from trusted timestamps and progressively increase clarity, not pressure.
- A compact showcase recalls the best shipped, evidenced capabilities available during the trial without inventing personal usage.
- Expiry copy separates paused protected behavior from retained identity and data.
- Suspended value uses only evidenced, shipped capabilities.
- The offer states governed price, cadence, provider path, and material limits exactly.
- Eligible restarts and applicable recovery actions remain discoverable.
- Lookup failures, pending review, refunds, revocations, and wrong-account states use diagnosis/recovery language rather than expiry persuasion.
- Founder voice may communicate care, commitment, and gratitude but never guilt, debt, abandonment, or product-survival pressure.
- The doctrine remains in the existing support-and-proof branch and is discoverable from the primary playbook and entitlement owner.
