# Progressive Clarity And Preserved Agency

## Outcome

Create one compact shared doctrine for sensitive product and operator interactions: increase clarity, consequence visibility, and recovery guidance with real stakes while preserving agency and limiting urgency to evidenced risk.

## Placement

- One canonical reference owns the general principle and pressure scenarios.
- Product critical moments discover it through the product decision chain.
- Operator decisions and recovery discover it through the partnership contract.
- Trial transition remains a specialized entitlement application.
- Ordinary low-stakes work does not load it, preventing activation and ceremony inflation.

The focused proof covers `PCA-001` through `PCA-008`, the state-to-recovery sequence, genuine-urgency evidence, progressive-disclosure limits, preserved alternatives, targeted discovery, and the entitlement specialization. The focused tests, metadata, dependency graph, skill audits, topology, and runtime sync passed on the recovery branch; PR #27 then merged the validated recovery into `main` as `bbc07fe`.
