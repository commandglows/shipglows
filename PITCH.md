# ShipGlows — Pitch

> Pitch reviewed: 2026-09-02 · Project state: see canonical sources below

ShipGlows is an open and inspectable business framework that helps humans and AI agents carry shared intent through identity, product, content, engineering, delivery, and proof. It is designed first for solo founders and small teams who need stronger judgment, continuity, and accountable execution across real projects.

## Current state

The framework, métier skills, governance corpus, CLI, TUI, and development-runtime capabilities are actively maintained and entering live use; the separate `shipglows_app` repository owns the managed SaaS product.
