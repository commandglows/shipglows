# SEO AI Visibility Review

Check whether intended AI crawlers can access server-rendered public content, robots policy, and useful `llms.txt`/full equivalents where appropriate. Review citation-ready structure, primary-source evidence, author/organization/schema truth, FAQ/HowTo only when semantically valid, consistent brand naming, and corroborating public docs. Do not promise crawlers, rankings, citations, or schema effects. Fresh official sources govern OpenAI claims; current attributable sources govern market/AEO claims.
