# Verification Coherence

Use only for applicable documentation, closure, skill, Atlas, product-decision, editorial, dependency, or cross-contract scope. Shared contracts are selected directly by the activation contract.

## Documentation And Closure

Align behavior/contracts with documentation or record an evidence-based no-impact verdict. Closure, changelog, archive, tracker, bug, spec, and source-of-truth state cannot claim more than implementation and proof. Internal ShipGlows contracts stay English; user-facing output uses the active language and correct French accents outside identifiers.

## Skill Coherence

For changed `skills/*/SKILL.md`, verify `Trace category` and `Process role`; chantier sources retain chantier-potential guidance; helpers do not claim source ownership; contract changes have scenario-first pressure proof or an explained exception; non-trivial behavior has proportional ZOMBIES coverage including justified not-applicable categories; runtime-discoverable changes pass the relevant sync check.

## Atlas And Product Decisions

Rerun Atlas protection against actual changed paths. Unapproved `block` fails; unresolved `review` prevents a clean protection claim. Compare actual artifacts and proof with accepted upstream need, journey, capability, critical moment, invariants, and cross-contract links. Material conflicts or orphan nodes fail coherence.

## Editorial Score

When required, validate score schema, signature, input refs, applied rules, status, evidence, confidence, and expiry. Reject stale/mismatched or recoverable non-final states. Only `ready`, `needs revision`, `blocked`, and `publishable with caveats` are final; any blocking criterion prevents ship-readiness.

## Dependencies And Risk

Inspect relevant new dependencies for necessity and vulnerability exposure plus obvious security, performance, data, destructive, migration, and public-claim consequences. Version drift is a warning unless it can change permissions, pricing, security, data behavior, public claims, or architecture; those cases are critical.
