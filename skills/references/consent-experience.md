# Warm Cookie Consent

## Activation And Ownership

Read when creating or changing a cookie-consent banner, preference panel, or its
copy and interactions. Experience owns the reusable intent; the active design or
development skill retains its task ownership. Unrelated authentication cookies
or newsletter consent do not trigger this reference.

## Lead With Care

Start with our sincere intention to offer the best possible experience thanks
to cookies. Speak as a welcoming team addressing a person, not as a technical
notice. Explain their useful contribution before describing settings: remembering
preferences, keeping a shopping basket, relevant recommendations, or learning
how to improve the service, according to the product's actual uses.

Cookies should not be presented as inherently negative. Preserve a positive,
caring voice rather than opening with defensive warnings, technical categories,
or a generic miniature legal notice. Distinguish the ambition to offer the best
experience from a claim that every cookie is indispensable. Name advertising
and tracking plainly when used; warmth must remain truthful.

## Approved French Tone Reference

Adapt this example to verified product behavior; do not copy unsupported benefits.
Preserve the caring opening and natural voice when translating or shortening it.

> **Une expérience pensée pour vous 💛**
>
> Grâce à nos cookies, nous souhaitons vous offrir la meilleure expérience possible.
> Retrouver vos préférences, vous proposer des contenus qui vous correspondent et
> rendre chaque visite plus agréable : ces petites attentions passent aussi par
> eux. Vous choisissez ceux que vous souhaitez nous laisser utiliser, et vous
> pouvez changer d’avis à tout moment.

## Design And Interaction

Use warm colors, a friendly illustration, and playful details when they fit the
brand. Make consent feel like a thoughtful part of the product. Keep copy easy
to scan, with accessible contrast, keyboard focus, responsive layout, and motion
that respects reduced-motion preferences. Do not require a mascot or emoji for
every brand.

Use explicit actions such as “Tout accepter”, “Tout refuser” and “Personnaliser”.
Keep refusal easy to find and use without guilt or extra friction. Explain what
each choice changes and provide a working way to revisit it.

## Delivery Proof

Check the rendered banner and preference panel, not only the text. Match each
benefit and purpose to actual cookie or service behavior. Verify acceptance,
refusal, customization, persistence, and reopening or changing the choice;
optional services must follow the recorded choice. Report any unverified behavior
without treating this editorial reference as a legal-compliance certification.
