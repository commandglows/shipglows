# Bounded Internal Reference-Register Updates

A supplied-link update stays in direct main-thread execution when one focused local lookup suffices,
public URLs/category are supplied, `candidate` is the only added status, no interpretation or
public claim is needed and duplicate/row proof suffices. Append factually under the original authority, without launching source intake, market study, or documentation topology work,
with one primary-source check per supplied reference. Otherwise normal routing and
approval apply.

`veille <URL>` analyzes without persistence; `concurrent <URL>` or `inspiration <URL>` updates its register; `veille` takes precedence.
