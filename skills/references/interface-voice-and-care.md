# Interface Voice And Care

Product interfaces, not infrastructure/agent reports. Experience owns intent;
active owners retain task/proof. Follow brand/user voice.

## Care In Context

Help the person succeed: lead explanations with intention/benefit, translate
state into their task, offer a concrete next step. Keep controls explicit;
technical detail optional unless decision-relevant. Beyond accuracy, make people
feel welcomed, encouraged, confident, recognized or supported through words/actions.

- Welcome/onboarding: connect a small action to their goal; encourage progress.
- Permission/setup: value first; allow defer/revisit; refusal is not failure.
- Empty: invite an achievable action; distinguish new workspace, no results and failure. Never invite creation when seeking existing work.
- Loading: say what we prepare for them; real progress, no invented duration or implementation jargon; unobtrusive repeated updates.
- Success: acknowledge accomplishment and next action; celebrate meaningful, brand-appropriate milestones.
- Error: calmly acknowledge interruption/consequence and offer working recovery; no blame or jokes about loss/failure.

Warm visuals/interactions may help; no mandatory mascots, emojis, confetti,
familiarity or repetitive praise. Preserve focus, contrast, reduced motion,
choice persistence and usable recovery.

## Truth And Proof

Express verified benefits and our intention to offer the best possible experience
confidently, without unsupported guarantees. Saved work, safe data, payment success,
no loss and harmless retry require evidence. Explain advertising, costs,
permissions and consequences plainly. Refusal/optional skip stays respectful and
neutral; danger requires a separate, explained real consequence. Preserve cookie
consent's benefit-led warmth.

Review rendered states for intent, emotion, natural brand voice, truth and
promised actions. Revise cold, blaming, patronizing or needlessly technical copy.
Verify respectful refusal, working recovery and actual completion. Reading this
or passing metadata checks proves neither application nor user experience.
