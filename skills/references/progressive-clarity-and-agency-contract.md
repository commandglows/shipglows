# Progressive Clarity And Preserved Agency

## Core Principle

As stakes, proximity, or irreversibility increase, increase clarity, consequence visibility, and recovery guidance. Do not manufacture emotional pressure to obtain action.

Pressure is justified only when it communicates a real, evidenced, time-sensitive risk. Its intensity must remain proportional to the consequence and deadline. Commercial desire, workflow convenience, lifecycle ceremony, agent confidence, or a preference for quick approval never creates legitimate urgency.

The intended progression is:

```text
state → consequence → value or reason → valid options → recovery
```

Preserve agency throughout: the person understands what is happening, what changes if they act or wait, which alternatives remain valid, and how to recover from failure or uncertainty. Clarity may become more direct; choices must not become misleading, obstructed, shaming, or artificially scarce.

## Application Gate

Apply this doctrine to moments where misunderstanding or pressure can materially change trust, consent, access, money, data, safety, or an operator decision. Typical surfaces include onboarding, permissions, authentication, errors, async work, trial transitions, paywalls, destructive actions, migrations, security warnings, approval plans, and blocked reports.

Do not load or cite it for ordinary low-stakes copy, routine implementation detail, or every skill invocation. Product journeys reach it through `product-decision-chain.md`; operator-facing decisions and recovery reach it through `operator-partnership-contract.md`. Owner contracts keep their specific truth, authority, legal, security, accessibility, and proof rules.

## Interaction Contract

1. **State:** identify the verified current state without conflating unknown, pending, failed, expired, denied, revoked, or completed conditions.
2. **Consequence:** explain what changes now, later, or not at all. Separate reversible inconvenience from irreversible harm.
3. **Value or reason:** explain why action may help, using evidenced product value, safety rationale, or operational benefit rather than fear or authority theatre.
4. **Valid options:** present every materially valid action with honest hierarchy. Do not hide refusal, postponement, restart, export, rollback, support, or recovery when the governing contract provides it.
5. **Recovery:** make retry, diagnosis, rollback, support, or safe continuation visible whenever failure or uncertainty is possible.

Use progressive disclosure to prevent overload: lead with the decision-relevant truth, then expose detail, diagnostics, or advanced alternatives when needed. Withholding a material consequence is not progressive disclosure.

## Genuine Urgency Boundary

Urgency may intensify only when current evidence establishes a meaningful deadline or escalating harm, such as credential compromise, destructive execution, data-loss risk, expiring recovery material, provider cutoff, or production incident. State the evidence, consequence, deadline, and safest action. Never exaggerate severity, certainty, affected scope, or permanence.

When evidence is incomplete, communicate uncertainty and the safest reversible next step. Do not convert uncertainty into alarm. When no real deadline or escalating harm exists, prefer calm specificity over countdowns, repeated interruption, punitive color, forced-choice language, or escalating notifications.

## Pressure Scenarios

- `PCA-001`: a low-risk onboarding choice reveals value and consequences progressively without front-loading warnings or forcing commitment.
- `PCA-002`: a permission request explains the capability enabled, the data boundary, refusal consequence, and later recovery before asking for consent.
- `PCA-003`: an async failure distinguishes delay, timeout, and terminal failure, then offers retry or recovery without implying lost work absent evidence.
- `PCA-004`: a destructive action increases directness in proportion to irreversibility and names backup/rollback truth; visual alarm does not substitute for consequence clarity.
- `PCA-005`: a genuine security incident uses urgent language only to the evidenced scope and deadline, with the safest action first and uncertainty explicit.
- `PCA-006`: a commercial transition showcases evidenced value and valid alternatives without fake urgency, guilt, concealed recovery, or invented personal usage.
- `PCA-007`: an approval plan explains scope, effects, proof, and alternatives without framing acceptance as the expected or loyal response.
- `PCA-008`: a blocked report exposes the real risk and safest continuation without dramatizing uncertainty or transferring internal workflow supervision to the operator.

## Proof Boundary

Review representative normal, uncertain, failed, time-sensitive, reversible, and irreversible states. Verify that urgency maps to evidence; material consequences and alternatives remain visible; recovery is reachable; claims stay inside product truth; and accessibility does not rely on color, motion, countdown, or tone alone. A calm-looking interface is not sufficient proof if it hides consequences or removes agency.
