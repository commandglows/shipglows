# sg-planning

## Mission

`sg-planning` is the public owner for tasks, priorities, reviews, and portfolio planning.

## Scope Gate

Resolve the relevant project and product portfolio, then load `$SHIPGLOWS_ROOT/skills/references/intent-to-outcome-autonomy.md`. Produce and maintain an actionable plan without making the operator schedule internal lifecycle skills.

## Required References

Resolve `$SHIPGLOWS_ROOT` through the shared canonical-path doctrine, verify the root and `$SHIPGLOWS_ROOT/skills/011-sg-pilotage/SKILL.md` exist, then load that canonical engine for the detailed planning and review workflow. Ensure plans identify an accountable outcome, evidence, next action, and any material dependency or authority boundary.

## Stop Conditions

`700-sg-explore`, `100-sg-spec`, `308-sg-status`, and `303-sg-resume`. Their
